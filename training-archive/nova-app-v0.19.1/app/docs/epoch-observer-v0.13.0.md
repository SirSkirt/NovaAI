# Neural Nova v0.13.0 — epoch observations, output practice and pathway contributions

The existing Visualize SVG/brain drawing remains visually and structurally unchanged, and stays at the top of its page. Below it is an observational pane that reads native app-private JSONL records from each unified and targeted training session.

Every completed epoch (even a checkpoint-rejected one) and epoch zero before training saves: the brain's greedy autoregressive generation (up to 24 characters) for one training practice example and one independently held-out example per available objective, teacher expected answer and exact-match/prefix diagnostics, full dense-layer activations, measured Transformer attention and bridge channels, recurrent update norms, two-way exchange activity norms, and the same-weight output-distribution ablation of (a) all dense context versus none, and (b) reciprocal exchange versus none. This uses the same actual Rust joint HybridBrain; no teacher answers are fed at inference and no fake "reasoning transcript" is produced. Green existing connector strokes indicate that removing the dense pathway causes a total-variation output difference above 1e-5 at the recorded prompt; they are not an absolute "reasoning" indicator.

Unified epoch records also include model step/fingerprint, current train/heldout losses, heldout accuracy, reasoning-preference rate, checkpoint-selection reason, prior-skill heldout and gradient/clip norms. Targeted records include candidate objective scores, focus and selection, so improvements and regressions from each attempted epoch can be inspected rather than seeing only the best candidate. The saved neural activations make the visualizer replayable without saving 60 entire model weight checkpoint files.

All observations are **read-only** and do not participate in the loss, neural memory, chat, optimizer, teacher data split, checkpoint selection or rollout answer generation. Up to 24 characters of generated output makes this a limited practice diagnostic, **not a formal unseen test** or proof of real reasoning. A neural latent state cannot be converted into Nova's literal inner thoughts. Some categories may have no independently held-out examples; the panel reports what actually exists. Sampling several responses and doing ablations every epoch costs CPU, especially on Android; verify performance/thermals on-device.

Native history commands accept only locally generated bounded session IDs; up to 40 recent sessions listed. Each session persists as one file under app data / neural-nova-epoch-observations /, bounded to 24 MiB. Reports may contain lesson examples; review before sharing. Epoch data is not a restorable .nhyb. No UI/brain architecture/signing/certificate or checkpoint-format change.

To validate: start a short unified mixed lesson and a targeted focused lesson, observe epoch-zero + every completed epoch on the Visualize tab, switch between epochs and inspect reported answers/ablation values; confirm bridge glows green only when measured no-dense output differs; confirm training/heldout split and selected epoch in logs; close/reopen app and refresh history. Run Rust, JavaScript, Windows, signed Android CI before distributing. Real-device runtime is a separate check.
